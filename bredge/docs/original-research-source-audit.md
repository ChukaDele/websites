## Source-grounded public analysis
- Nigerian Telecoms Q4 2025: Bredge's report gives 179.64M active voice and 148.17M active internet subscriptions and identifies NBS/NCC as source. Primary source: https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/179/related-materials. Subscriber connections are not a count of unique people. A porting balance cannot be presented as overall churn.
- Petrol Price May 2026: Bredge's report gives a national mean of NGN 1,596.25 per litre, Edo NGN 1,722.91 and Adamawa NGN 1,469.83. Primary source: https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/157/related-materials. Historical surveyed prices must not be advertised as today's pump prices.
- Cost of a Healthy Diet April 2026: Bredge's report gives a national mean of NGN 1,589 per adult per day, Ekiti NGN 2,036 and Adamawa NGN 1,143. Primary source: https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/146/related-materials. NBS reporting shows approximately 3.12% month-on-month and 4.74% year-on-year, while the Bredge PDF shows 3.11% and 4.68%. Those rates remain excluded pending source reconciliation.
- Inflation July 2026: a branded report contains the 15.43% July 2026 CPI rate and claims 18 checks performed. The July report and validation log were not independently recovered from the NBS catalog. Keep this report out of the initial live-page source set until its primary source and audit trail are verified.

The new Research page identifies these as Bredge-authored analyses of public datasets. It shows the period, original NBS source, method context and limitations. No client results, endorsements, private contracts, confidential data, or fabricated savings are inferred from these public-data figures.

## Required approval before publishing full branded PDFs
1. Content owner confirms ownership and permission to distribute the report layout, underlying datasets and derived analysis.
2. Each figure is reconciled with the precisely dated original NBS release and calculation, with differences corrected.
3. All imagery/third-party assets and any Power BI exported views are cleared, with personal and client-sensitive information removed.
4. Original byline, publication date, data period and source credits are agreed; no false current-data framing.
5. PDF exports receive accessible HTML equivalents, accurate alt text/tables and canonical permanent URLs.
6. Final reviewer confirms responsive design, source links, and the live contact conversion before any protected production release.

This task implements a reviewable text-based Research route and homepage entry, without uploading or publishing the original PDFs or the PBIX files.
