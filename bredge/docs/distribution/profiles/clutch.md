## Reviews (the main lever — do it right)
- Invite **genuine, recent, eligible** clients only. Clutch verifies each review by
  phone or an online form, so reviewers must be real and willing to be contacted.
- **Never** self-write, incentivise for positive sentiment, pay for, fabricate, or
  sentiment-gate reviews. Ask a representative set, not only your happiest client.
- Track: platform · client (private) · invited date · published? · verified URL
  (per `seo-backlinks-distribution.md` §H).
