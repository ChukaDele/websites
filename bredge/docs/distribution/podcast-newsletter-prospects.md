# The Bredge — Podcast & Newsletter Prospects (brief §19)

30 relevant data-engineering / analytics / BI podcasts and data / BI / operator
newsletters, plus tailored outreach drafts for the top 10. Every show and publication
below was verified to be real and active (checked around Aug 2026); no invented shows or
URLs.

**Spokesperson framing (all pitches):** The Bredge speaks from the **consulting side** —
a practitioner who has seen the same data problems across many growing / mid-market
companies. Vendor-neutral, synthetic examples only, no tool pitch. Best subjects:
revenue/identity reconciliation, data reliability & quality, "what to fix first", the
first data hire, warehouse readiness, reporting automation, and Power BI performance.

> **Verification rule:** the "recent episode/article" column is accurate as of Aug 2026.
> Before sending, re-open the show/newsletter and cite the **actual latest** relevant
> episode or issue — never reference one you have not re-checked that week.
